Privacy policy

The Big Tuna - Guitar Tuner · Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

Big Tuna does not collect, store or share any personal data, from anyone, children included. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can hear your instrument. It listens only while the tuner or intonation-check screen is open and the app is in the foreground. The sound is analyzed on your device, in real time, to find its pitch. It is never recorded, saved to storage or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (selected tuning, calibration and preferences), any tunings and setlists you create, and whether you have unlocked Big Tuna Pro are saved on your device so the app remembers them. They are never sent to us. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, like any app's settings; otherwise they are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Big Tuna Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been purchased on your account, so it can unlock the Pro features and restore them on a new device.

Network

The app may download app updates from our update service (Expo). That request carries no personal information beyond what any network connection reveals, such as your IP address.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. If you send a message through the support page's contact form, it is delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, and we use your email address only to reply.

Contact

Questions? Send a message from our support page.
