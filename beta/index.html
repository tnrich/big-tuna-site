Test Big Tuna on Android

The Big Tuna - Guitar Tuner · free Android beta

Google Play only lets a new app launch after at least 12 people have tested it for 14 days. If you have an Android phone and a guitar, bass or ukulele (or just want to help), joining takes about two minutes. The app is free.

  1. Join the tester group. Open groups.google.com/g/big-tuna-testers and tap Join group. Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone. You won't get any emails from it.
  2. Opt in. Open play.google.com/apps/testing/com.thebigtuna.guitartuner and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install, on your phone. Open Big Tuna on Google Play on your Android phone, or search "The Big Tuna" in the Play Store app. This step won't work in a computer's browser: Google shows test apps only in the Play Store app, so a laptop gets a "not found" page. It can take a few minutes after opting in before the Install button shows up.
  4. Keep it for 14 days. Tune up a few times, try other tunings and instruments, and leave it installed. Please don't leave the test early: Google counts every day.

Found a bug or have an idea?

On the app's Play Store page, scroll to Private feedback to developer, or send a message from the support page. Anything helps, including "it worked fine."

Questions

The opt-in page says the app isn't available. Make sure you joined the group with the same Google account you're signed in with. Right after launch, Google's review of the beta can take a day; check back.

The Google Play link says "not found". You're probably on a computer. Open it on your Android phone instead, signed in with the account you opted in with.

Who sees my email? Only the developer. Other testers can't see the member list.

iPhone? Not needed for this test; Big Tuna is coming to the App Store too.
